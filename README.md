## 📐 Section 1: Mathematical Definition of Pressure

### The Sabermetric Leverage Index ($\text{LI}$) Formulation
Traditional metrics erroneously define pressure as simply "Death Overs (16–20)" or "Required Run Rate > 10". In blowout matches where Win Probability is $< 0.1\%$ or $> 99.9\%$, death overs possess zero outcome variance. 

True pressure occurs when a single ball creates maximum Win Probability swing. We formalize this using the **Leverage Index**:

$$\text{LI}_t = \frac{\mathbb{E}_{\text{outcome}} \left[ | V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t) | \right]}{\mathbb{E}_{\text{all}} \left[ | \Delta V | \right]}$$

- **High-Pressure Cutoff:** Top 25th percentile ($\text{LI}_t \ge 1.50$), meaning every delivery swings match win probability by at least $1.5\times$ the historical T20 baseline.

### Statistical Validation (Welch's Two-Sample t-Test)
We conducted formal hypothesis testing across all **200,999 deliveries**:
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** Run and wicket distributions under $\text{LI} \ge 1.50$ are identical to normal play.
- **Empirical Result:** 
  $$\mathbf{t = -36.12}, \quad \mathbf{p = 3.07 \times 10^{-281}}$$
- **Behavioral Shift Under Pressure:**
  1. **Wicket Hazard Rate:** Surges from $3.76\%$ in normal play to **$11.62\%$** under pressure (**$3.09\times$ higher**).
  2. **Boundary Seeking Rate:** Surges from $15.36\%$ to **$29.36\%$** (**$1.91\times$ higher**).
  3. **Run Variance per Over:** Increases by **$+142\%$** due to the high-stakes boundary-or-wicket gamble.

### The 10-Factor Multi-Dimensional Rating System
To prevent single-metric distortions, each player's composite score aggregates 10 orthogonal high-leverage dimensions:
1. **$M_1$ WPA per Ball:** Direct win probability shift added per delivery.
2. **$M_2$ RAE per Ball / Econ Savings:** Runs Added Above Expectation relative to match par.
3. **$M_3$ Lone Warrior / Lone Enforcer:** Relative output when batting/bowling partners fail.
4. **$M_4$ Steep Chase WPA / WAE:** Performance in steep chases ($\text{RRR} \ge 12.0$ in overs 14–20).
5. **$M_5$ Boundary Surge / Dot Containment:** Ability to hit boundaries or bowl dots when field is spread.
6. **$M_6$ Dot Ball Resistance / Boundary Suppression:** Preserving scoreboard momentum.
7. **$M_7$ Wicket Preservation Delta / Death Overs WPA:** Maintaining wickets in hand or taking death wickets.
8. **$M_8$ Era-Adjusted Par Performance:** Normalized against 2024–2026 hyper-scoring baselines.
9. **$M_9$ Par Score Deviation / Narrow Defense Impact:** Performance defending $\le 12$ runs in last 2 overs.
10. **$M_{10}$ Match Impact Consistency Rate:** Percentage of high-leverage matches with positive value added.

### Empirical Bayes Shrinkage Formulation
To guard against small-sample noise, raw composite scores $\hat{\theta}_i$ with $N_i$ deliveries are shrunk toward the global mean $\mu_0$:

$$\tilde{\theta}_i = B_i \hat{\theta}_i + (1 - B_i) \mu_0, \quad \text{where } B_i = \frac{\sigma_0^2}{\sigma_0^2 + \sigma_i^2 / N_i} \in [0, 1]$$

High-volume performers (Jasprit Bumrah, $N=411, B_i=0.89$) maintain their raw scores, while moderate-sample performers (Ayush Mhatre, $N=97, B_i=0.52$) are conservatively shrunk toward replacement level.

## 🛡️ Section 3: Fair Comparisons & Eligibility Auditing

1. **True IPL Pressure Filter (The Ripon Mondol Audit):**
   - Imposed strict requirement of **$\ge 30$ high-leverage deliveries faced/bowled directly in the IPL**.
   - Eliminates regional tournament anomalies (e.g. Ripon Mondol who had 0 IPL balls) in favor of proven performers (e.g. Bhuvneshwar Kumar with 466 IPL pressure balls).
2. **Competition Quality Weighting:**
   - $\lambda_{\text{IPL}} = 1.00$, $\lambda_{\text{T20I}} = 0.85$ to account for international bowling quality variances.
3. **Empirical Role Classification:**
   - Separated genuine batters (positions 1–7, career bowling $<10\%$ overs) from bowlers (bowling in $>75\%$ appearances), eliminating misplaced all-rounders from pure batting ranks.
